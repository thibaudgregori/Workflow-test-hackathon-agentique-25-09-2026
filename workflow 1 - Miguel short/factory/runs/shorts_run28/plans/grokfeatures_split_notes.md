# grokfeatures split: author notes (2026-09-23)

I built the plan and the sealed scene as they stand. I did not change the module on disk. These notes cover where the split had to add something, and where I would have done things differently.

1. **Connector declarations were added in the generator, not the module.** `#arrow-cal` carries only `data-connect-to`. The geometry audit's `visual_laws.py` refuses a connector that has no `data-anchor-side` or `data-check-at`. Without an arrowhead marker it also measures the shaft end (x 685), not the painted tip (x 702). `declare_connector()` makes exact single replacements in the emitted string. It adds `data-anchor-side="left" data-anchor-fraction="0.5" data-check-at="14.2"` to the div and `data-connector-head` to `path.ah`. Nothing about geometry, colour or timing changes. After this the strict audit reports 0 errors. The design agent should add these declarations to the module next time.
2. **The emphases are not declared as `data-emphasis`.** Both are border or outline flips on the object itself (`#t-grok` border, `#box .bxo` stroke). `visual_laws` needs an emphasis element that is separate from its target, and there isn't one here. LAW 38 rule 2 is met by the flip itself.
3. **LAW 42: `#box` is on screen for 44 % of the runtime (14.84 to 30.28).** The plan declares it as the carrier of seam 3: the open box becomes the first parcel of the pile, per LAW 45's second remedy. The generator exempts it by name (`CARRIERS`). The longest mark that is not the carrier is on screen for 24 %. I would have let it leave at 29.80 with the pile, but the plan already does that under the sheet.
4. **GRAPHIC CHART clause 5 vs the scene:** the module sizes the mark ink at 74 px in 112 px tiles (0.66). The chart says 0.50 (56 px). The scene is sealed, so I left it as is. The marks read clearly at phone size.
5. **Captions:** I removed the phrase break at 12.70 s ("features | to their", a 0.36 s breath), and the pill may not break between "Grok" and "Build". The result is "24 major features to their" | "Grok Build application.". Without this, the partition stranded "to their", a two-word beat of function words.
6. **Composition placement:** at k = 1, top 192, the content band covers canvas 280 to 726. That leaves about 280 px of empty cream above the drawings, and it follows the handoff's placement. I would have centred the band in the top zone (0 to 862) instead.
