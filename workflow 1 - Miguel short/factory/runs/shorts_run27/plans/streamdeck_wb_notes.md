# streamdeck whiteboard: notes against the plan

1. The plan draws the flashlight beam as a "pale terracotta cone of light". The whiteboard brief does not allow solid fills on drawn objects, so the board draws the beam as two terracotta edge strokes, with three short terracotta glow ticks at the lens. Nothing else changed.
2. The keypad is a card-filled body in the plan's scene. On the board the body and keycaps are open marker outlines with no fill, for the same reason.
3. Emphasis kind "box" on the fridge, TV, keypad and keycap is built as the board's border flip: the object's own outline is retraced in terracotta, tagged `data-emphasis="outline"` with its target. It is not a `box_emphasis()` rectangle, because the plan asks for the border flip.
