# streamdeck split: author notes

## One law-driven departure (format-side, the sealed module is untouched)

LAW 30 (right rail, critical readable annotations) forbids ink past x 918 between
y 30 % and 95 %. The sealed scene centres ONE CLICK on the big key at core x 845, so
its final K reached about x 924 at canvas y 640-684 (33-36 %). The split moves the
big key AND its label 8 px left together on the emitted string: a static left
offset, no motion. The weld stays exact (the label is still centred on the key), the
gutter under the key base stays 28 px, and in the browser the label box now ends at
x 918.0, which includes the trailing 1.2 px letter-spacing. The cutout sits at k < 1,
so it does not need this. What I would have done instead: author the chapter-4 group
(prompt 110..500, key 720..970) about 10 px further left in the module itself.
