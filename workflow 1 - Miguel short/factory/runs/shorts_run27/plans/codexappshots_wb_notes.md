# codexappshots whiteboard: notes from the whiteboard author

The plan was built as written: five chapters (erases at 7.24, 13.52, 17.48 and 24.94), the rising sheet at 29.94, key term APPSHOTS first at 25.6 u, all six keys BELOW their objects, the one c1 connector landing on the tile's left anchor, and zero pointing cues. Every object is a `b.stroke()` marker path. `b.shape()` is used only for the two registry marks and the chapter groups.

## Where a law overrode the plan

1. **LAW 4, the double-caption guard.** The whiteboard chunker cut two bare pills that read exactly like the plan's keys while those keys are on the board: `AI assistant.` (12.58-13.52) against AI ASSISTANT, and `two command keys` (14.90-16.28) against TWO COMMAND KEYS. `caption_identity_guard` refused the build. The keys stayed as the plan names them. In my own `_captions` override, the trailing function words of the previous beat move forward: `might need` / `to your AI assistant.` and `Simply click on` / `the two command keys`. Widths are re-measured and stay under the 756 px budget. Nothing is shrunk. The pills now carry the sentence, and the board carries the name.

## Small additions (not disagreements)

- **Chapter 3 arc arrow.** The whiteboard_version asks for "an arc arrow carries it to the Codex mark". It is declared as a second connector (`c4-arc`, to `c4-codex`, ending on `anchor_points(TILE_C4, 1, "left")`). It fades at 22.08, together with the absorbed polaroid.
- **Emphasis as a retrace.** Both box-kind emphases are the object's own outline retraced in terracotta by the marker (the command caps 14.90-16.86, the laptop bezel 18.44-19.84), then rubbed out. This follows the plan's whiteboard_version and the brief's rule that a state change is a second ink colour. `box_emphasis()` was not used.
- **Phone-at boxes** are this board's own seats, from the rendered layout, at instants where the drawing is complete: hat 2.6, footprints 9.9, keys 16.0, polaroid 20.4, piggy bank 27.4. They are not the plan's canvas bboxes.
