# warpgrok: whiteboard author notes

Built the plan. These are the places where the whiteboard departs from the letter of a `whiteboard_version` field, with the reason for each.

1. **The row slides on "released" (16.84). It is not erased and redrawn.** The three hung keys, hooks 1-3 and PROVIDERS move one step left together (-105 core px over 0.50 s, SWING). This is the same move the split and the cutout make, so LAW 51 holds across all three lanes. The plan's own open question already says the whiteboard "may" erase and redraw. That option would redraw three keys, three hooks and a label in the 0.40 s before "new feature", and it would have pushed hook 4 later than its word. The Claude key's move on "multiple" (1.06) is also this same slide, matching the other lanes.
2. **There is no small key glyph on the outro sheet.** The outro is the shared harness' opaque rising sheet (`whiteboard_build.outro_block`), and its card is fixed: terracotta rule, handle and `daily AI`. It has no slot for a themed glyph. Adding one would mean forking the harness, which the brief forbids. What I would have done: add a small plain key above the rule.
3. **The Grok key is drawn onto hook 4 in place (19.42 to 19.62).** It does not drop and swing, which matches the plan's whiteboard version ("the Grok key on it"). GROK is written from 19.62 to 19.84, inside the 1.0 s label window. The Grok head's terracotta retrace starts at 19.90, not 19.70, because the one marker finishes GROK first. It is still inside the spoken word "subscription" (19.70 to 20.36).
4. **The loose keys use the sealed scene's placement.** The angles are Claude +16, OpenAI -6 and Gemini -17 degrees, taken from `LOOSE` / `K1_ALONE_DX` in `gen/warpgrok_scene.py`. The plan's draft listed -18, +5 and +16. The handoff says the scene's boxes are authoritative, and matching them keeps the lanes identical.
5. **Emphasis is a retrace, and the colour returns to ink by fading the retrace.** Each emphasis is the object's own jittered line redrawn in terracotta over itself (the board form of the border flip). A fade back to ink was not written in the plan: plank retrace 11.00, head retraces 13.70, both over 0.24 s. The Grok head and the second plank flip hold until the sheet covers them.
