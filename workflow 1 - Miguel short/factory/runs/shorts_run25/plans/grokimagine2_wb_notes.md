# grokimagine2 — WHITEBOARD author notes (departures from the plan, built anyway)

The plan's lane, four bespoke objects, four output icons, eight keys (all BELOW
their objects), chapters and erase instants (5.80, 13.00, 17.86, outro 28.24),
four level connectors, the one step-2 emphasis, the Grok cast and the zero
pointing cues are built as written. Departures:

1. **No outro glyph (o-glyph, the small wand). A law forbids it on this lane.**
   `assert_outro_clear` refuses any rigid at or after the outro anchor, and the
   outro is the shared harness's fixed opaque rising sheet (rule, mono
   @migueltorrez.ai, daily AI). Drawing the wand there would mean forking the
   harness. What I would have done: the plan's small wand on the sheet if the
   harness took a glyph parameter. The split and cutout lanes still carry it.

2. **The palette is drawn a little higher and smaller in chapter 0 (132 x 92 u at
   y 216-308) than a lone hook would be.** The plan says the palette never moves
   or shrinks on the board. For that to be true, it has to sit where chapter 1
   still has room for the bus, four icons and their keys under it before the
   caption band (426 u). What I would have done: a larger centred palette that
   shrinks upward at 5.80, as the split does.

3. **In chapter 3 the Grok tile lands over the picture (left of centre), not at
   the top centre.** The plan's whiteboard version puts the picture left of
   centre from the start and welds the tile to it (block
   grok-tile-2/picture/selection/key-any-image). A tile at the top centre with
   the picture on the left would break that block. It travels 17.86-18.36 as the
   handover object.

4. **The step-2 emphasis is a terracotta retrace of the step's own outline,
   declared `data-emphasis="outline"` (target `podium-step2`, check-at 15.46).**
   The plan's kind is "box", but in the page contract a `box` must clear its
   target by 4 px, and the plan wants the outline itself recoloured, with no new
   geometry. A clearance box would also collide with the Grok tile standing 4 u
   above the step. It is erased at 17.40 (lifetime 14.56-17.40).

5. **The Grok tile on step 2 is drawn in (tile stroke plus mark pop) at 14.36; it does not
   drop from above.** The marker draws. This is the whiteboard's own way to make
   things appear.

6. **Captions, LAW 4:** the chunker produced a pill reading exactly
   "infographics," while INFOGRAPHICS is written on the board. The same words are
   re-cut as "infographics, images," | "videos, anything that" | "you might need."
   | "It's also classed". Every pill is inside the 756 px budget.

7. **The podium digits 1/2/3 are written at 13.34-13.50, as soon as the podium is
   complete,** which is before "two" (14.36). The plan says the steps are
   numbered. They are the shape's content, not labels.
