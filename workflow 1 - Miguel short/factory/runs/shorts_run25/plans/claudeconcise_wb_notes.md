# claudeconcise whiteboard: where the board departs from the plan's letter

The plan was built as written: three chapters (erase at 13.22 and 29.78), the same four bespoke objects (jargon speech bubble, long paper scroll, style selector dial, scissors cutting the scroll), the same seven keys at the same beats and placements, two box_emphasis (dial on the turn, short note on 'understand'), and the dial carried across the second seam. No plan instruction was refused. The departures below are small and each has a reason.

1. **Outro glyph left out.** The plan asks for the lockup to land on a small ink glyph of the short note. The shared harness's outro is a fixed opaque sheet (rule, handle, `daily AI`), and `assert_outro_clear` refuses any ink authored at or after the outro anchor. Adding the glyph would mean forking the harness, so the lockup is the standard chassis one.
2. **The dial's right tick stays ink, not terracotta.** Beat 3's terracotta tick is a DOM-lane instruction. On the board, the terracotta box around the dial is the dial's one accent, and `visual_laws` refuses an emphasis that shares a colour with its target's ink. The tick is drawn heavier instead, and CONCISE is printed beside it.
3. **The ANTHROPIC card starts at 13.30, 0.24 s before 'Anthropic' (13.54).** LAW 45 requires a complete object within 0.30 s of the chapter-0 erase finishing. The card is drawn inside the erase and is complete at 13.72.
4. **The whiteboard pointer is redrawn, not rotated.** This follows the plan's `beats[4].whiteboard`: the old grip ridge is erased and a new one is drawn at the CONCISE tick.
5. **UNDERSTOOD sits 3 u left of the paper's axis.** LAW 12's right rail (x 489.6 u below y 307.2 u) is measured on the clip-reveal bound (0.70 em), not on the glyph ink. The key is still below the note and inside LAW 39's band (offset −3.1 u against a ±46.8 u band).
6. **The scissors cut at y 312 u, not the scroll's midpoint.** This keeps the handle loops clear of the Claude Code to scroll arrow on the chain row (y 280). The chain arrow still lands on the short note above the cut, as the plan's connector note requires.
7. **Captions regrouped at 15.36-18.26.** The chunker emitted a lone pill 'Output Styles.' at the moment the board writes OUTPUT STYLES (a LAW 4 double). The same words are now cut as 'allows you to' | 'change that.' | "It's called Output Styles." (727 px against the 756 px budget, none of them function-only).
8. **The phone-at boxes are the board's own positions of the plan's four bespoke objects.** The plan's normalised bboxes describe the split's seating, and on this board they would crop empty cream.
