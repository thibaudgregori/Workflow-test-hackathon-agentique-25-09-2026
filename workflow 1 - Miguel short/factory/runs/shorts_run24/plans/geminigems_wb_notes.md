# geminigems — WHITEBOARD author notes (disagreements with the plan, built anyway)

The plan's argument, lane, objects, keys, sides, chapters, emphasis and cast are
built exactly as written. Five departures, each with its reason; four are forced
by a LAW or by the shared harness, one is a judgement call inside a beat.

## 1. The outro glyph (`o-glyph`, `o-gem`, `o-rule`, `o-slot`) is NOT drawn — LAW

The plan's beat 6 asks the rising sheet to carry a small parcel with the gem
dropping into its mouth on the word "migrate". A LAW forbids it on this lane:
`whiteboard_build.assert_outro_clear` refuses any board rigid whose `t0` is at or
after the outro anchor ("Nothing may be drawn into the region the handle card
occupies"), and the whiteboard's outro is authored by the shared harness
(`outro_block`), which is fixed: opaque cream sheet, terracotta rule, mono
`@migueltorrez.ai`, `daily AI` micro-line. Drawing the glyph would mean forking
the harness, which the brief forbids. What I would have done: exactly the plan,
if the harness took a themed-glyph parameter — that is a harness change, not a
per-video one. The split and cutout lanes still carry the glyph, so the idea is
not lost from the day's set.

## 2. The gem is RE-INKED at the left across the first seam, not slid there

Plan (beat 2, whiteboard_version): "the gem is not redrawn". The board is
CHAPTERED, and the chapter-0 board is wiped at 5.52; the harness has no primitive
that moves a drawn group AND re-registers its rigid geometry for the geometry
laws, so a slide would leave every chapter-1 gutter measured against a box the
viewer no longer sees. LAW 45 also wants a complete nameable object inside the
erase: the same gem, re-inked smaller at the left from 5.56 and closed at 5.79,
satisfies it directly. The gem is the same silhouette drawn the same way (LAW 51),
just smaller — the viewer reads it as the same stone.

## 3. The parcel DOES travel across the second seam — and its SKILLS key does not

Plan (lifetimes): `key-skills` travels with the parcel to 17.52. The parcel does
travel (that is LAW 45's second sanctioned handover and it is what the plan
wants), but the written key stays behind at the 10.46 erase. Reason:
`assert_label_law` builds its registry of written type as `{TEXT: t0}`, so a
second rigid named `type:SKILLS` at the far side of the seam OVERWRITES the first
and reports the key as written at 10.9 — 3.7 s from the word "Skills" (7.16) that
the plan pins it to, which fails the label law's `LABEL_WINDOW`. The key is
therefore written ONCE, beside the parcel, on its own word, and the parcel
carries its meaning across the seam as an object the viewer watched get named
eight seconds earlier. What I would have done: travel both, if the label registry
were keyed by rigid identity rather than by text.

## 4. The right-hand connector is drawn at 12.36, not at 10.90 — LAW 24

Plan (lifetimes): `conn-left` and `conn-right` both from 10.9. He says "either
with your teammates" at 10.46 and does not reach "with members of your
community" until 12.18. A terracotta line pointing at an empty right half for
1.5 s is content that has not been spoken yet, which LAW 24 (NO PEEK-AHEAD)
refuses. `conn-left` is drawn at 10.90 as planned; `conn-right` is drawn on
"members" (12.36), inside the same beat, and the crowd follows at 12.86.

## 5. The crowd's back row is heads only

Plan: "seven smaller bust figures in two staggered rows, the back row half hidden
behind the front". Ink-line drawings have no fill, so a full bust behind a full
bust is not hidden — it is seven transparent outlines crossing each other, and on
the page it read as a tangle of circles rather than as people. The back row is
three heads riding in the gaps above the front row's four busts: seven people,
two staggered rows, nothing hidden that a viewer must un-cross. Measured on the
page before any render.
