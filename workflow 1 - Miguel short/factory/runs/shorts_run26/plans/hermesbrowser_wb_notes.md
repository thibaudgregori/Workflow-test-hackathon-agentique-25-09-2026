# hermesbrowser whiteboard: notes against the plan

The plan was built as written (three chapters, seams 3.58 and 11.74, the browser as the carried anchor, the same three objects, the same seven keys, the same two outline flips). Where the whiteboard differs, it is for one of these reasons:

1. **No binoculars glyph on the outro sheet.** The plan puts a small binoculars glyph on the rising sheet. The shared harness builds the outro (sheet, rule, handle, `daily AI`), and `assert_outro_clear` refuses any ink authored at or after the outro anchor. Adding the glyph would mean forking the harness. The whiteboard outro is the harness's standard sheet, the same one every approved whiteboard ships with.
2. **The Hermes arrow sits at the tile's mid-height, not at the browser's mid-height.** The plan asks for "the Hermes tile's left edge (mid-height) into the browser's right edge at the same height" and also cites `anchor_points(browser, 1, 'right')` (the browser's mid-height). Those two cannot both hold on this board, because the tile sits below the question bubble. The arrow is horizontal at the tile's mid-height (y 243.9 u) and lands on the browser's right edge. That still meets LAW 40: it is a single connector, its end is on the target rectangle, and it is declared.
3. **Label size is 17 u (32 px), not 28 px.** The board is drawn at 1.875 px per unit. At 15 u (28 px) the verb row read small at phone size. All seven small keys share the one size and each sibling row shares one baseline (LAW 50). The key term is 25.6 u (48 px), which matches the plan.
4. **The second HERMES AGENT is registered as `type:HERMES AGENT [tile]`.** The harness keys its label law by text, so two identical keys would collide, and the key-term-first check would read the chapter C label's time. The generator adds its own LAW 4 overlap check for that key against the real pills. It passes: no pill reading "Hermes agent" is on screen from 18.50 to 22.30 s.
5. **Caption re-cut (LAW 4).** The chunker emitted a lone pill `main browser,` at the moment the board writes MAIN BROWSER. The two pills are re-cut on the same words as `that browser as` | `your main browser,`.
6. **`--phone-at` boxes are this board's own object boxes**, not the split's plan bboxes. The whiteboard draws the objects in a different place, so the plan's boxes would crop empty board. The times are the plan's (8.4, 9.3, 10.4).
