# Format Lab review — Miguel, 2026-08-30

First review round of the 6-format × 3-variant lab. Every remark below becomes a
format law or a global law. Verbatim intent preserved; fixes tracked per format.

## GLOBAL LAWS (apply to ALL formats, including the existing split)

1. **ZOOM CAP (sleek framing).** "Overall I think you do zoom in way too much on
   my face. Ideally we shouldn't have to zoom so aggressively — not just my
   face, my entire torso and such can also be in the video." The face crop in
   use is too tight; wider framing with shoulders/torso is the default for
   face-led formats. Punch-in percentages must be DERIVED, not guessed: extract
   the frames just before and after each zoom, measure what fraction of frame
   height the head occupies, and cap it. (Derivation task open — facesplit fix
   round.) The takeover's zoom *style* (hard cut punch-ins) is the preferred
   grammar over animated creep.

2. **SFX LAW v2.** Effects in the lab were (a) unpleasant in character, (b) too
   LOUD relative to voice and bed, (c) sometimes slightly out of sync with the
   on-screen event. New rule: SFX sit clearly under the voice (level to be
   pinned on the measured instrument in the fix round), the family is soft/tactile
   rather than sharp, and every SFX is frame-locked to the visual event it
   scores. The whiteboard marker squeak at shipped volume is a hard NO.

3. **NO SQUARE-ENDED FILLS IN ROUNDED CONTAINERS.** "The straight bars at the
   end of rounded edge boxes is a big no no... UGLY AS FUCK... across multiple
   generations... that's a big no even for the formats and variants that already
   exist." Any fill/progress/highlight inside a rounded-corner container must be
   clipped to the container's radius — never terminate in a hard straight edge.
   Audit the EXISTING factory chassis for this too.

4. **NO PEEK-AHEAD.** Content that has not been spoken yet must not be visible
   (artifact spine: next section peeking at the bottom edge was distracting —
   e.g. the 05 → 06 Nous Research transition). Reveal on speech, not before.

5. **NO UNNECESSARY MOVES.** Camera/scroll movements that aren't motivated by
   the narration are cut (artifact v3's unmotivated move from 06-Nous Research
   up into the card above). If a move has no spoken reason, it doesn't happen.

6. **25→30fps JUDDER (root-caused).** Source face footage is 25fps conformed to
   30 by duplicating 1 frame in 6 (measured: 17% duplicates). Invisible when the
   face is small; visible stutter when full-frame/zoomed. Face-led formats
   render at native 25fps until recordings are 30/60fps. Recommend OBS capture
   at 30 or 60fps going forward.
