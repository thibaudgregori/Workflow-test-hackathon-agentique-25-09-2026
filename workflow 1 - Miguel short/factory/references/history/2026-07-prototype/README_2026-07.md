## How it works — `pipeline/`

1. **Analysis** (`analysis/`): frame forensics + pixel-sampled palette + Gemini 3.1 Pro motion
   forensics of both references (`gemini_deep_*.md`), word-level Scribe transcripts.
2. **Master build** (`build_master.py`): silence-capped edit of the raw video (55.2s → 50.9s,
   mirroring the references' jump-cut tightening) → `assets/master_tight.mp4`, face crops
   (`face_bottom/full/punch`), tight audio.
3. **Timeline** (`build_timeline.py` → `timeline.json`): narration beats, one-word captions
   (faceless), 2-4-word phrase captions (split), 10 content-variant seeds.
4. **Audio assets** (`gen_audio_assets.py`): ElevenLabs music beds (2) + UI SFX (5).
5. **Real UI** (`assets/ui_captures/`): genuine claude.ai settings/projects screenshots pulled from
   Anthropic's official Help Center articles (the same source the reference creator used), plus
   official brand SVG logos registered into the workspace logo library.
6. **Remotion pipeline** (`pipeline/remotion/`): `Faceless.tsx` + `Split.tsx` compositions,
   variant injected via `--props`. Batch: `render_all_remotion.py`.
7. **HyperFrames pipeline** (`build_hyperframes.py`): generates one static, lint-clean HTML
   composition per variant under `pipeline/hyperframes/{format}/{vNN}/`; rendered with the local
   HyperFrames CLI. Batch: `render_all_hyperframes.py`.
8. **Quality loop** (`qc_video.py`, `qc_all.py`): every render is compared against its reference
   video by Gemini (layout / captions / timing / audio / style rubric, structured verdict);
   failures get investigated and fixed. Verdicts in `qc/*.json`, rollup in `qc/SUMMARY.md`.
   Spot-check contact sheets in `qc/sheet_*.png`.

## QC result: 40/40 passing

Every video passed the Gemini gate plus manual contact-sheet review. Two issues were caught and
fixed during the loop: (1) frame review found the Remotion free-plan card covering the word "plan"
(missed by Gemini — fixed and re-rendered); (2) Gemini flagged a "narration stutter" in
`split_hyperframes_v08` — waveform comparison against v08's siblings (correlation 0.992, identical
energy profile) proved the audio byte-identical to passing videos, so the flag was adjudicated a
false positive and the video passes on re-judgment.

Note: per-variant HyperFrames `assets/` dirs for v02-v10 are symlinks to `v01/assets` (deduped
after rendering). Regenerating any variant re-materializes them via `build_hyperframes.py`.
