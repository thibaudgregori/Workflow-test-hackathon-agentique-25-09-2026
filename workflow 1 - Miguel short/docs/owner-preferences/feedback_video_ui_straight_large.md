ghost numerals, spotlight/blurred backgrounds, terminal chrome, serif-poster, typographic-hero.

**Why:** he wants clean professional layouts where the REAL UI is the hero — straight, large,
unrotated, minimal or no card chrome (S06's bare UI card went furthest and made the cut). Structure
should come from typography and progress indicators, not decorative frames around screenshots.

**How to apply:** in any shorts/video layout work, present product UI flat and big; use serif
numbers, titles, checklists, progress bars for structure; skip device mockups and tilt/rotation on
screenshots unless he asks. Real UI footage (official demo videos or scripted Playwright captures)
beats static mockups.
