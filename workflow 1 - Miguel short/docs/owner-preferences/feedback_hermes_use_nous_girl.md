Whenever Hermes needs to appear in a thumbnail or visual, use the Nous Research girl mascot, never the `hermes-agent` H glyph.

**Why:** Miguel's audience does not recognize the H mark, and it renders faint/low-contrast against light backgrounds — it reads as an artifact rather than a brand.

**How to apply:** Registry entry `nous-girl` is the default — its source PNG is a framed illustration on an opaque white box, and Miguel picked exactly that look over a transparent cutout when shown both (2026-08-05). A transparent black line-art variant also exists as `nous-girl-line` (frame cropped, white matted to alpha) if a no-box version is ever wanted; do not substitute it by default.
