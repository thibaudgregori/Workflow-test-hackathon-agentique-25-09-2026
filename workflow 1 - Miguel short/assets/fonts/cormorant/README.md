# Cormorant (SC and Garamond)

Classic Garamond-style serif by Christian Thalmann. Cormorant SC (small caps) was the text font of the original Canva wedding site; Cormorant Garamond (variable weight, roman and italic) is its lowercase partner for longer reading.

- Source: Google Fonts repository (`ofl/cormorantsc`, `ofl/cormorantgaramond`), downloaded 2026-09-25.
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL.txt`).
- CormorantSC-Bold.ttf added 2026-09-26 (same source) for the Our Love Story weight options.
